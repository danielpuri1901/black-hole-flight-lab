## Shared interface

`cabin.js` exports `createCabin(THREE)` returning `{group, update(frame), dispose()}`.
It uses the scene coordinates in the spec.
`cockpit-3d.js` installs `window.CockpitRenderer` after its imports resolve.
The renderer constructor accepts the existing canvas and preserves `render(frame)` and `dispose()`.
Additional methods: `setView(name)`, `moveLocal(dx,dy,dz)`, `interact()` optional.
Frame additions: `fuel`, `burn` as a two-element normalized command, `reducedMotion`, `effectsTime` in seconds.
`lookYaw` and `lookPitch` are unrestricted yaw and bounded pitch in radians supplied by the UI.
Positive movement dz means toward the windshield; renderer applies the camera-relative vector and cabin bounds.
View presets are `pilot`, `cabin`, and `window`.
The renderer exposes a readable `mode` and initialization errors without replacing scientific outcomes.
HTML starts the pilot module only after the renderer module has loaded, with explicit 2D fallback on module/renderer failure.
