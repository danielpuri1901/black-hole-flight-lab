Mission rules and replay

The safety cutoff is r = 2.1 M, outside the horizon at r = 2 M. Reaching it ends the mission as a failure. Escape requires an outward coasting state at r = 20 M with energy above 1. A flight that reaches its time limit is unresolved.

The replay interpolates saved calculated states in proper time. Burn markers show recorded decisions. The command display uses the local radial and tangential components, not a direction on a distant sky map. The camera uses approximate Schwarzschild light tracing with cinematic orientation and artistic disk emission. It does not model the view from an exact comoving powered camera, tides, radiation, or a survivable spacecraft.

Units: G = c = M = 1. Looking around changes the camera only. Launch settings and the controller change the calculated flight.

The learned pilot

A generator.
A check. A new burn.

An action autoencoder compresses twelve two-axis burn commands into eight numbers. A conditional diffusion transformer generates plans from the current ship state. Checked AI scores sixteen learned proposals in the known powered physics. Hybrid also offers classical fallback plans. The model learns from teacher plans; these mission results use separate held-out launches.
