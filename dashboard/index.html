What this flight means

These are unpowered particle orbits around a non-rotating black hole. Energy and angular momentum set the launch. Units: G = c = M = 1. Launch radius: 30.

Capture stops at r = 2.001, just above the horizon. This does not model radiation, tides, or a powered spacecraft.

The validation check flags launches near the learned barrier using potential error on separate validation observations. Abstain means the heuristic rejects a raw prediction. Outside the band does not certify safety.

Inspect exact certificate

The kernel counts turning roots with exact rational arithmetic and a Sturm sequence. It checks this unpowered Schwarzschild launch. This is an exact check under these assumptions, not a Lean proof.
